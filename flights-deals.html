How to find a better fare

  • Be flexible. Shifting your trip by a day or two can reduce the price.
  • Try nearby airports. A different airport in the same region may have better fares.
  • Book early for busy periods. Holidays and school breaks fill up fast.
  • Compare total cost. Include baggage and seat fees, not only the base fare.
