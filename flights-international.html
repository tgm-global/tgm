Before you fly abroad

  • Check entry rules. Passport validity, visas and health requirements vary by country and can change.
  • Name must match. Your ticket name must match your passport.
  • Plan connections. Long-haul trips often involve a connection. Allow extra time if you change airports or terminals.
  • Review baggage. International fares can have different baggage rules from domestic ones.
