Tips for renting a car

  • Pick the right size. Count passengers and bags before choosing a category.
  • Check fuel and mileage rules. Some rentals limit daily mileage.
  • Understand insurance. Check what your card or personal policy already covers.
  • Airport or city pick-up. Airport locations are convenient but may add fees.
