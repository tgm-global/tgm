Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information bookflights.online ("we", "us") collects, why we collect it and how we use it. By using our website you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Details you send us. When you submit an enquiry or contact form, we receive your name, email address, phone number, trip details (such as route, dates, travellers and preferences) and any message you write.
  • Technical data. Our servers and hosting provider may record basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.
  • Cookie choice. We store your cookie preference in your browser. See our Cookie Policy.

How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you travel options by phone or email.
  • To answer questions and provide support.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent spam or abuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

Form submissions are delivered to us through a third-party form service (Web3Forms). If you go on to book, we may share the details needed to complete your booking with the relevant travel provider or partner. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

We keep your details only for as long as needed to handle your enquiry, meet legal requirements and resolve disputes.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to certain uses. To make a request, contact us.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. Please do not send payment card numbers or passwords through our forms.

Children

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
