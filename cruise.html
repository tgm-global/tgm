Before you choose a cruise

  • Pick a region. Popular choices include the Caribbean, Alaska and the Mediterranean.
  • Check the season. Weather and prices vary through the year.
  • Understand what is included. Meals are usually included. Drinks, excursions and gratuities may not be.
  • Plan how you get to the port. Arrive the day before if you can.

Need to reach the departure port? Check flights, or ask about a package. First time sailing? Read our travel blog.
