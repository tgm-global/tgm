Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in the same way. In this policy we call them all "cookies".

How we use cookies

  • Essential. Needed for the website to work and to remember your cookie choice. These cannot be switched off.
  • Optional. If we add analytics or marketing tools in the future, they will only run if you choose "Accept all". Today the site does not need them to function.

Cookie choice

When you first visit, a banner asks for your choice. We store that choice in your browser so we do not ask again each time. You can change it at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer of any page.

Third-party services

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google to download them, and Google may receive technical data such as your IP address. Form submissions are handled by a third-party form service. See our Privacy Policy.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block all cookies, parts of the website may not work as expected.
