Planning a domestic trip

The United States has a dense domestic network, so many city pairs have several flights each day. Departure times, connections and fares change with the season, the day of the week and how early you book.

  • Compare nearby airports. Large cities often have more than one airport.
  • Consider travel days. Midweek flights are often cheaper than Friday or Sunday flights.
  • Allow time for connections. If you need to change planes, leave enough time between flights.
