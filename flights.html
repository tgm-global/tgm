How flight requests work

Enter your route, travel dates, number of travellers and cabin class. We pass your details to our team, who come back with available itineraries and fares by phone or email.

The home page search covers routes between 100 US cities. For international trips, choose a destination on the international page and send us your dates.

Tips before you request

  • If your dates are flexible, tell us. Moving a trip by a day or two can change the fare.
  • Check baggage rules for your fare. They differ by airline.
  • Make sure the name on the booking matches the traveller's ID or passport.
