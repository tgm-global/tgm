Choosing a hotel

  • Location first. A hotel close to your main plans can save time and transport costs.
  • Check what is included. Breakfast, parking and Wi-Fi are not always part of the rate.
  • Read the cancellation terms. Flexible rates usually cost more but give you options.
  • Book flights and hotel together. See our holiday packages.
